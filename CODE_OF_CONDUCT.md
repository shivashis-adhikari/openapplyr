# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and specific, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted anywhere in the project's spaces.

To report a problem, use **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull request and choose to report it to the repository's maintainers. Reports are read only by the maintainers, and the person reported does not see who reported them. Maintainers may edit or remove content and block people who break this code.
